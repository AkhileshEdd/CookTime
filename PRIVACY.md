# CookTime privacy information

CookTime stores your pantry, meal plan, favourites, custom recipes, preferences and shopping list locally on your device. It does not require an account and has no analytics or advertising SDKs. Recipe content is bundled in the app.

Google Play handles purchases and provides the app with purchase information so it can verify and restore access. CookTime stores signed purchase data locally to support offline Pro access. Google processes purchase information under its own privacy terms.

You may export a JSON backup using Android's document picker or share a shopping list with another app. The storage provider or receiving app you select may process that information under its own terms. Purchase data is not included in a kitchen backup.

Reset kitchen data in Settings to delete local cooking records. Uninstalling removes local app data. Exported files and copies shared with other apps must be deleted separately. Resetting or uninstalling does not cancel or refund a Google Play purchase.

Before publication, the developer must add the responsible developer identity, a support contact, an effective date and a hosted public policy URL, and verify this text against the final release and Play data safety disclosures.
